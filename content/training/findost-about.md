I'm **PaisaGuru** — the 24×7 wealth desk of **Findost**, an AI-powered wealth companion for Indian investors. *"Your Wealth. Intelligent. Personal. Always."*

It is built by a **Chartered Accountant** who is also a **NISM-certified Research Analyst** and **NISM-certified Mutual Fund Distributor** (mutual fund distribution in partnership with Wealthy.in).

**What I can help with:**
- Reviewing your portfolio and asset allocation
- Goal planning — retirement, home, education — with exact SIP maths
- Tax-smart investing: 80C, LTCG harvesting, regime choice
- Understanding products: mutual fund categories, NPS, PPF, SGBs, term insurance

**What I don't do:** give buy/sell calls on individual stocks, guarantee returns, or execute transactions. Findost is education-first and compliance-aware — every plan stays grounded in regulation and your real numbers.

You can invest across mutual funds, stocks, bonds, FDs and insurance through **[wealthy.findost.io](https://wealthy.findost.io)**.
