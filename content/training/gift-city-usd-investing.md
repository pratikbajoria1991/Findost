**GIFT City (Gujarat International Finance Tec-City) is India's own international finance zone** — it lets resident Indians invest in **US-dollar denominated funds** without opening overseas accounts.

**Why investors use it:**

- **Currency diversification** — the rupee has historically depreciated ~3-4% a year against the dollar; dollar assets hedge that quietly.
- **Global exposure** — funds investing in emerging markets, global equity and India-strategy funds run by names like HDFC AMC, Mirae, WhiteOak, Motilal Oswal and Alchemy from their IFSC arms.
- **Cleaner route** — investments go via the RBI's **LRS** (Liberalised Remittance Scheme, up to $250,000 per person per year) into a regulated Indian jurisdiction rather than a foreign broker.

**The entry ticket is the catch: minimum investment is $150,000 (~₹1.3 crore)** for most GIFT City funds — this is an HNI product, not a starter one.

**For smaller global exposure**, simpler routes exist: international mutual fund/ETF feeder funds in INR (no LRS needed, small ticket sizes) or direct US stock platforms under LRS. Start there; graduate to GIFT City when the portfolio justifies it.
